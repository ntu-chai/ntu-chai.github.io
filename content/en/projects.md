## Pillar 1

### AI Humanities Tool Sets
Focused on research automation: precise document guidance and material organization for the individual scholar.

### Aim
This pillar aims to develop tools customized for individual research fields across the liberal arts college. For example, we might develop tools to assist in the collection of oral-history data to surface previously undocumented Indigenous settlement patterns in mountainous Taiwan. Alternatively, we can construct tools to analyze Classical Chinese, Buddhist, Indigenous, and Japanese-era archives within a single diachronic embedding space.

### Tool Set Items
- Auto-summarization
- Citation tracing
- Domain-specific data curation
- Domain-specific data analysis

## Pillar 2

### Agentic digital humanities
An agent team for the humanities that augments the hermeneutic loop, covering the full life-cycle of cross-departmental research.

### Aim
This pillar aims to model and scale the hermeneutic loop by deploying a collaborative network of specialized AI agents. Rather than just automating tasks, these agents will actively participate in the iterative process of interpretation, critique, and refinement across diverse humanistic traditions. By bridging departmental boundaries, this agentic team will support the full lifecycle of complex, cross-departmental projects, moving seamlessly from individual analysis to interdisciplinary collaboration.
### Agents for hermeneutic loop of humanities research
- Editor agent
- Critic agent
- Expert agents

## Pillar 3

### Trusted scholarly compute
Three families of academic computation that underpin the agents above, embeddings, small specialised models, and multimodal humanities AI.

### Aim
This pillar aims to extend embedding research through semantic manifolds and diachronic geometry while establishing specialized small language models (SLMs) tailored for Classical Chinese, Buddhist texts, and specialized domains like IOL (International Linguistics Olympiad) reasoning. Furthermore, we construct multimodal humanities AI architectures capable of analyzing and connecting diverse, cross-medium historical archives.

### Examples of each category
- A.  Embeddings research
    - semantic manifold
    - diachronic geometry
    - glyph embeddings
- B.  Small specialised models
    - Classical Chinese model
    - Buddhist semantic
    - IOL reasoning
- C.  Multimodal humanities AI
    - manuscripts, rubbings, inscriptions
    - paintings, historical maps
    - oral-language audio archives
