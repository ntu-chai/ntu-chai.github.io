## Computational Macroscopy

### Digital Remote Sensing × Indigenous Settlement Detection
With Sarah Parcak (UA). Satellite + multispectral imagery, paired with
linguistic and oral-history data, to surface previously undocumented Indigenous
settlement patterns in mountain Taiwan.

### Anthropology × Multimodal AI
Field tablets for cultural codes — embedded with the lab's multimodal models,
deployed alongside anthropologists in long-term field engagements.

### Deep Time Semantic Model
Classical Chinese, Buddhist, Indigenous and Japanese-era archives as a single
diachronic embedding space. Joint work with NTU Library and the Department of
History.

## Living Lab

### Generative Curation
Responsive gallery prototypes — the room rearranges its own narrative as the
audience moves through it. Joint work with ZKM Karlsruhe and the CHAI Arts
Festival programme.

### Digital Ethnography
Field methods for AI-mediated culture. Joint work with SOAS, UoL and Leiden
University.

### CHAI Arts Festival
Annual public showcase. Programmed by the Living Lab residency cohort. First
edition Oct 2026.

## AI Alignment Sandbox

### Reward-function ethics encoding
Pluralist value frames — Confucian, Buddhist, indigenous Taiwanese — encoded
directly into RLHF reward functions. Joint work with Stanford HAI.

### Philosopher-in-the-loop audit toolkit
Open-source. Designed and tested with the Department of Philosophy.

### Cross-domain Lab
Joint code review with the College of EECS — every alignment artefact ships
through both reading rooms.
