This workshop traces one arc, from research workflows built on Claude to the local computing resources humanities researchers can actually draw on. The morning opens with Shu-Kai Hsieh's overview of the lab and of Claude, followed by a hands-on session with Po-Ya Angela Wang on system setup and the paper reader. In the afternoon, Da-Chen Lian introduces local computing options for humanities AI, and the day closes with open discussion and a wrap-up.
